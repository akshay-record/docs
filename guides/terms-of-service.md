## 6. Skill Verification Process

**6.1 Human Endorsement**

Candidates may request skill verification from designated professional contacts, including reporting managers, functional or technical leads, clients or stakeholders, founders or executives, and peers. Each endorsement request is specific to a combination of skill, source, and endorsement type. Each combination constitutes one attempt. Both approved and rejected responses are recorded permanently in the system and cannot be revised or retried for the same combination.

Candidates must not submit false, fabricated, or misleading information about their skills or sources. Candidates must not misuse the endorsement system by submitting fake verifier email addresses, requesting self-verification through alternate identities, or harassing or spamming verifiers with repeated requests.

**6.2 Assessment Verification**

Candidates may verify skills through AI-generated proctored assessments. Assessments are designed to evaluate practical knowledge and are aligned to the candidate's mapped sources such as work experience, projects, or certifications. Assessments are conducted in a controlled environment with proctoring signals including webcam monitoring, audio detection, and activity tracking.

Any form of cheating, use of external assistance, tab switching, or screen capture during an assessment is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate disqualification and account suspension. Reattempts for assessments may be permitted based on platform-level rules that are communicated to you at the time of the attempt.

**6.3 Skill Bundling**

Candidates may bundle up to four skills in a single verification request sent to the same verifier. Bundling is optional. Each skill within a bundle is evaluated independently by the verifier, and the outcome of one skill does not affect the outcome of others within the same bundle.

**6.4 Automatic Account Creation via Job Board Integration**

When you use a job board or staffing platform that has integrated Record's skill verification infrastructure, clicking the skill verification option within that platform will initiate an automatic account creation process on Record. The integrated platform will share your registered email address and relevant profile data stored on that platform, which may include your name, work experience, projects, and certifications, with Record for the sole purpose of setting up your Skill Vault account and pre-populating it with your existing profile information.

This process is initiated only upon your explicit action of clicking to verify your skills on the integrated platform, and occurs with the consent you provide at that point. By initiating the skill verification flow on any Record-integrated job board or platform, you acknowledge and agree that your profile data from that platform will be shared with Record to create and configure your Skill Vault account.

Once your account is created, you can access your Skill Wallet directly through Record using the same email address associated with your job board account. Any skills you verify through the integrated platform will be stored in this Skill Vault and will be accessible and portable across all other platforms that integrate Record's technology.

If a Skill Vault account already exists under the same email address, the verification will be linked to your existing account automatically. No duplicate account will be created. Your existing verified skill records will remain intact and the new verification will be added to your existing profile.

You may log in to your Record Skill Wallet at any time using the email address associated with your job board account to view, manage, or share your verified skills independently of the platform through which your account was originally created.

## 8. Skill Wallet and Data Portability

The Record Skill Wallet is a candidate-facing product that stores all verified skills in one place, regardless of which platform or job board the verification was initiated through. Verified skills are tied to the candidate's email identity and are portable across all platforms that integrate Record's technology.

Candidates may link multiple email addresses to their Skill Wallet through the Connected Accounts feature to consolidate skills verified across different platforms and identities. Each connected email address is verified through a one-time password process before it is linked. Any skill verified under a connected email address will automatically appear in the Skill Wallet.

Disconnecting an email address will stop future skill syncing from that identity but will not delete previously synced verified skill records. Candidates who wish to remove previously synced records must submit a separate deletion request to [support@userecord.io](mailto:support@userecord.io).
