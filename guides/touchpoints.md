---
title: "Verification Touchpoints"
description: "How verification proofs accumulate across endorsement types and assessments."
---

Each source supports up to 10 unique skills. Each skill can accumulate up to 6 independent verification proofs, giving a maximum of **60 touchpoints per source**.
