Record Technology Labs Private Limited<br />Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the Record Playground (the dashboard you sign in to) uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). For cookies on our website userecord.io, see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy).

## 1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files saved on your device when you use a website. In this policy, "cookies" also covers similar technologies such as local storage.

## 2. Types of cookies we use

**Strictly necessary cookies (always active).** These are needed for the Playground to work. They keep you signed in, support Google sign-in, protect your account and remember your cookie choices. You cannot turn them off. We do not use them to track you on other websites.

**Functional cookies.** These remember your choices so the Playground stays the way you left it, for example the organisation and project you last worked in, and whether you were in Live or Test Mode.

**Analytics cookies.** These help us understand how the Playground is used, such as which pages and features people visit, how they move through the product, and where something goes wrong. We use Mixpanel, a third-party analytics service, for this. Analytics cookies are on by default. You can turn them off at any time.

We do not use advertising cookies in the Playground.

## 3. Third parties

Mixpanel sets analytics cookies when analytics is on. Google sets cookies when you sign in with Google. Each has its own privacy and cookie practices, available on their websites.

## 4. How long cookies stay on your device

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Other cookies stay for a set period, which varies by cookie, unless you clear them earlier.

## 5. Your choices

You can change your choices at any time from the cookie settings link in the Playground footer. If you turn analytics off, we stop sending data to Mixpanel from your browser.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop parts of the Playground from working, including sign-in.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top.
