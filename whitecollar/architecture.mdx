---
title: "Architecture"
description: "High-level architecture of the AI Assessment Portal."
---

```
Parent App (Client)
    |
    +-- POST /api/v1/sessions        <- Creates session with candidate list
    |   (x-api-key + x-secret-key)
    |
    +-- Embeds creator modal iframe  (?token=xxx&company=xxx)
    |
    +-- Candidates receive email link -> record-assessment-frontend

Creator Modal (white-collar-assessment-portal)
    | Step 1: Job Details   Step 2: Config    Step 3: Generate
    | Step 4: Review        Step 5: Send      Step 6: Success

Candidate App (record-assessment-frontend)
    | /assessment/:id           <- Email + OTP verification
    | /assessment/:id/verify    <- Identity photo verification
    | /assessment/:id/start     <- Exam with proctoring + recording
    | /assessment/:id/complete  <- Results + AI feedback
```
