## Step 1 — Job Details

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `jobTitle` | Yes | Custom error if empty |
| `level` | Yes | junior / mid / senior |
| `skills` | Yes | At least 1, max 3 |
| `jobDescription` | No | Optional, max 1500 chars |

## Step 2 — Configuration

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `questionRows` | Yes | Per-type count & marks — must total 100 |
| `difficultyLevel` | Yes | easy / medium / hard |

## Step 3 — Generating (drum-scroll loader)

- Understanding Your Requirements
- Building AI-Powered Questions
- Creating Knowledge Assessments
- Generating Coding Challenges (hidden if no coding)
- Preparing Your Assessment
