## Frontend validation

- Total of count x marksEach must equal 100
- Submit blocked with error banner until balanced
- Auto balance button proportionally scales to 100

## Default configurations

| Type | Without Coding | With Coding |
|---|---|---|
| MCQ | 10 x 2 = 20 | 10 x 2 = 20 |
| True/False | 2 x 5 = 10 | 2 x 5 = 10 |
| Short Answer | 5 x 6 = 30 | 4 x 5 = 20 |
| Long Answer | 4 x 10 = 40 | 2 x 10 = 20 |
| Coding | — | 2 x 15 = 30 |
| Total | 100 | 100 |
