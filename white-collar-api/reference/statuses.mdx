---
title: "Statuses"
description: "The states of a job mandate, its questions, a campaign and a candidate."
---

## Job mandates

| `status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `live` | Takes campaigns. |
| `closed` | No new campaigns, and its running campaigns are paused. [Reopen](/white-collar-api/guides/job-mandates#close-and-reopen-a-job) it to continue. |

## Questions

`questions.status` on a job mandate:

| `status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `none` | The job has no questions yet. |
| `ready` | The questions match the job and the assessment settings. A campaign can be created. |
| `outdated` | The job or its assessment was changed after the questions were written. Write or save the questions again before the next campaign. |

## Campaigns

| `status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | Nobody has been invited. |
| `sending` | Started, and some candidates are still waiting for their invitation. |
| `sent` | Everyone has been reached. Some invitations may have `failed` (see `candidates.failed`); starting the campaign again retries them. |
| `paused` | New invitations are held until you resume. |
| `completed`, `cancelled` | Finished. |

## Candidates

`status` on a candidate row, a result and an event:

| `status` | Meaning | Announced as an event |
|---|---|---|
| `pending` | Added, not invited yet. | No |
| `sent` | Invited, has not started. | No |
| `in_progress` | Started the assessment (or verified their identity and is about to). | No |
| `completed` | Submitted. | **Yes** |
| `timed_out` | Time ran out. | **Yes** |
| `disqualified` | Disqualified. | **Yes** |
| `failed` | The invitation email could not be delivered (`reason: undeliverable`). | **Yes** |

`verdict` is `hire` when the score is at or above the pass mark, `reject` below it, and `null` while there is no decision.
