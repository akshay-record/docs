---
title: "Limits"
description: "Rate limits and size limits."
---

Limits are a token bucket per key and endpoint class, plus a whole-key bucket (and a per-recruiter bucket when you send `x-on-behalf-of`). Every answer carries `RateLimit-Limit`, `RateLimit-Remaining` and `RateLimit-Reset`; a `429` adds `Retry-After`.

| Endpoint class | Used by | Per second (burst) | Per minute | Per day (IST) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `workflowWrites` | Workflow writes | 5 (10) | 60 | 2000 |
| `mandateWrites` | Job mandate and question writes | 10 (20) | 300 | 20,000 |
| `campaignWrites` | Campaign writes | 2 (5) | 30 | 500 |
| `reads` | Every read, events and webhooks | 20 (40) | 1200 | none |
| `wholeKey` | All calls of the key together | 150 (300) | 6000 | none |

A **TEST** key gets one tenth of every number. 100 rejections inside a minute put the key in a 60-second cooldown, doubling on repeats within the hour (up to 15 minutes). Record can raise the limits for a key.
