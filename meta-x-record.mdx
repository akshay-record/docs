# Meta WhatsApp Business Configuration Guide

This guide explains how to configure your Meta WhatsApp Business Account for integration with the Record Platform.

To enable WhatsApp communication through your own WhatsApp Business Account, a few Meta configurations are required. Once these configurations are completed, the Record implementation team will connect your WhatsApp Business Account with the Record Platform.

---

## 1. Information Required from Your Team

Please share the following details after your Meta WhatsApp Business Account has been configured.

| Required Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Phone Number ID | The Phone Number ID associated with your WhatsApp Business number. |
| Permanent Access Token | A permanent access token generated from your Meta Business Account. |
| WhatsApp Business Account ID | Optional, but helpful for verification and future support. |

---

## 2. Generating a Permanent Access Token

To allow the Record Platform to securely send WhatsApp messages on behalf of your WhatsApp Business Account, you must generate a **Permanent / No Expiration Access Token** from your Meta Business Account.

<Note>
  We recommend generating a dedicated access token specifically for the Record Platform. This makes future management, auditing, and permission control much easier.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meta Business Settings">
    Sign in to your **Meta Business Manager** and navigate to:

    ```text
    Business Settings → Users → System Users
    ```

    Here, you can either:

    - Create a **new System User** specifically for the Record Platform. This is recommended.
    - Use an existing System User if one has already been configured for your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the WhatsApp Application">
    After selecting the System User, ensure that the appropriate WhatsApp application is assigned.

    You have two options:

    - **Option 1 — Recommended:** Create a dedicated application for the Record Platform.
    - **Option 2:** Use your existing WhatsApp Business application if you prefer to manage all integrations within the same project.

    Both approaches are supported. However, using a dedicated application provides better separation of environments and simplifies future maintenance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Permissions">
    Before generating the token, confirm that the System User has the required permissions for the assigned WhatsApp Business application.

    The System User should have access to:

    - WhatsApp Business Account
    - WhatsApp Phone Number
    - Application Management
    - Messaging Permissions with full access

    These permissions are required for the Record Platform to send WhatsApp messages, manage templates, and receive delivery status updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the Access Token">
    Once the application has been assigned:

    1. Select the **System User**.
    2. Click **Generate Token**.
    3. Choose the WhatsApp application that will be integrated with the Record Platform.
    4. Select the required permissions.
    5. Generate the token.

    When generating the token, choose a **Permanent / No Expiration Access Token**.

    This ensures uninterrupted communication between your WhatsApp Business Account and the Record Platform without requiring periodic token updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the Token with Record">
    Once the token has been generated, securely share the following details with the Record implementation team:

    - Permanent Access Token
    - Phone Number ID
    - WhatsApp Business Account ID, if available

    Our team will securely configure these credentials within the Record Platform to complete the integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 3. Best Practice for Access Token Setup

For improved security and easier management, we recommend creating a **dedicated System User** and a **dedicated application** exclusively for the Record Platform.

This approach provides several benefits:

- Isolates Record Platform access from other business integrations.
- Simplifies permission management.
- Makes future token rotation easier, if required.
- Prevents disruptions to other WhatsApp integrations within your Meta Business Account.
- Provides better operational control and auditability.

<Note>
  If you already have an existing WhatsApp application in production, you may continue using it by generating a new Permanent / No Expiration Access Token for the Record Platform.
</Note>

## 4. Webhook Configuration

Please configure the following webhook in your Meta Developer Console.

### Callback URL

The Record implementation team will provide this URL during onboarding.

```text
<RECORD_CALLBACK_URL>
```

Example:

```text
https://your-domain.com/api/v1/webhooks/whatsapp
```

### Verify Token

The Record implementation team will also provide the Verify Token.

```text
RECORD_VERIFY_TOKEN
```

### Webhook Events

Please subscribe to the following events:

- `messages`
- `message_status`
- `message_template_status_update`

Once completed, click **Verify and Save**.

## Steps - Meta Developer Account Configuration

To connect your WhatsApp Business Account with the Record Platform, complete the following steps in your Meta Developer account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Meta Developers">
    Sign in to your Meta Developer account using the Meta Business account associated with your WhatsApp Business Account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Your App">
    Navigate to your application dashboard.

    From the left navigation menu, open the **Dashboard** and locate the **Use Cases** section.

    Select **Connect with customers through WhatsApp** to begin the WhatsApp Business setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Production Setup">
    During the production setup, Meta will ask for your webhook configuration.

    Enter the following details provided by the Record team:

    - Callback URL — shared by the Record implementation team during onboarding.
    - Verify Token — shared by the Record implementation team during onboarding.

    After entering both values, click **Verify and Save** to complete the webhook configuration.

    <Frame>
      ![Image](/images/image-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register Your WhatsApp Business Number">
    Once the webhook has been successfully verified, complete the remaining WhatsApp Business setup by:

    - Registering your WhatsApp Business phone number.
    - Completing the business verification process, if applicable.
    - Adding your payment method for WhatsApp conversations, if required by Meta.
    - Completing the phone number registration.

    Once these steps are complete, your WhatsApp Business Account will be ready for integration with the Record Platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create WhatsApp Message Templates">
    After your WhatsApp Business Account is fully configured, create the required WhatsApp message templates in your WhatsApp Business Manager.

    We recommend creating templates for the following supported languages:

    - English
    - Tamil
    - Telugu
    - Hindi
    - Kannada
    - Malayalam

    Once Meta approves your templates, share the **approved template names** with the Record implementation team.

    The Record team will map these templates within the platform to ensure the correct language template is automatically used while communicating with candidates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the Required Details with Record">
    Once your setup is complete, share the following information with the Record implementation team:

    - Phone Number ID
    - Permanent Access Token
    - Confirmation that the webhook has been successfully configured
    - Approved WhatsApp template names for all required languages

    After receiving these details, the Record implementation team will complete the platform configuration, connect your WhatsApp Business Account with the Record Platform, perform end-to-end testing, and notify you once your integration is ready for production use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 7. Best Practices

Use these guidelines if you are adding your own message in the WhatsApp template.

To improve message delivery and candidate engagement, we recommend the following:

- Keep messages conversational and friendly.
- Avoid phrases such as **Profile Verification** or **Employment Verification**, as they can resemble phishing or KYC scam language.
- Use branded messaging wherever possible.
- Avoid exposing technical query parameters in links outside of Meta Call-to-Action buttons.
- Always include an opt-out message.
- Keep templates concise and easy to understand.
- Use localized language for each supported region instead of direct word-for-word translations.

<Warning>
  Avoid sending raw URLs with visible technical parameters such as workflow IDs or candidate IDs in the message body. Use a Meta Call-to-Action button wherever possible.
</Warning>
