A campaign sends your candidates (name + mobile number) to an enrichment. Record contacts them on WhatsApp, follows up by call and SMS, and asks the enrichment's questions.

| Needs | You get | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| `enrichment_id` from [step 1](/enrichment-api/guides/enrichments) | `campaign_id` | `enrichment:campaigns` (reads also allow `enrichment:read`) |

<Warning>
  **This contacts real people and spends credits.** There is no separate start step: contacting begins as soon as the campaign is created. The phone numbers in the samples are placeholders, so replace them with your own test numbers. If the enrichment's `questions_status` is not yet `ready`, the campaign waits and goes out by itself.
</Warning>

## Candidates

- `phone_number` is an Indian mobile number: 10 digits (`98765 43210`) or with the country code (`+91 98765 43210`). It is returned in full as `+91…`.
- `external_id` is your id for the candidate. It comes back on every candidate and result.
- Up to 1,000 candidates per call.
- **A row that cannot be contacted never fails the call.** It is left out and listed in `skipped` with its position and a reason: `invalid_phone`, `opted_out` (the number replied STOP earlier) or `duplicate` (already earlier in the same list). If no row can be contacted, the call answers `400` and `details` say why for each one.
- A TEST key holds at most 50 candidates per enrichment (`403 FORBIDDEN` beyond that).

## Campaign status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sent` | Live. Candidates are being contacted. |
| `paused` | Paused by you. Nothing more goes out. |
| `completed` | Finished. |
| `cancelled` | Stopped for good. |

`awaiting_questions: true` means the questions are still being prepared and nothing has gone out yet.

## Endpoints

### Create a campaign

`POST /enrichments/{enrichment_id}/campaigns`

Returns `campaign_id`, the candidates and the skipped rows.

**Scope:** `enrichment:campaigns` · **Idempotency-Key:** required

<Warning>This contacts real people.</Warning>

| Field | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | `string` | **Required.** 1–200 characters. |
| `candidates` | `array<object>` | **Required.** 1–1,000 of `{ name, phone_number, external_id? }`. |
| `language` | `string` | Invite language. Defaults to the enrichment's schedule language. |

### List campaigns

`GET /enrichments/{enrichment_id}/campaigns`

The campaigns of an enrichment, newest first, with `counts` of candidates, sent and completed. Takes `limit` and `cursor`.

**Scope:** `enrichment:read`

### Get a campaign

`GET /enrichments/{enrichment_id}/campaigns/{campaign_id}`

Progress of one campaign: the funnel, numbers per channel and where candidates drop off per question.

**Scope:** `enrichment:read`

### Pause a campaign

`POST /enrichments/{enrichment_id}/campaigns/{campaign_id}/pause`

Nothing more goes out (no invite, follow-up, SMS or call) until you resume. Only a live campaign can be paused.

**Scope:** `enrichment:campaigns` · **Idempotency-Key:** optional · **Errors:** `403 FORBIDDEN`, `404 NOT_FOUND`, `409 CONFLICT` (the campaign is not live)

### Resume a campaign

`POST /enrichments/{enrichment_id}/campaigns/{campaign_id}/resume`

Sending carries on where it stopped. Only a paused campaign can be resumed.

**Scope:** `enrichment:campaigns` · **Idempotency-Key:** optional · **Errors:** `403 FORBIDDEN`, `404 NOT_FOUND`, `409 CONFLICT` (the campaign is not paused)
